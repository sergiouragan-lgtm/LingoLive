Business Case

✅ Opportunities

  • Competitive Advantage: Full-duplex voice with natural interruptions vs competitors' turn-based systems
  • User Engagement: More natural conversations expected to increase session duration +5-10%
  • Market Expansion: Support for 8+ regional dialects (PT-BR, PT-PT, ES-ES, ES-MX, etc.)
  • Quality Improvement: GPT-4 backbone vs custom ASR expected to reduce errors

⚠️ Risks & Mitigation

  • Cost: 2.5x current cost → Mitigation: Negotiate volume rates with OpenAI
  • Latency: 328ms vs 150ms baseline → Mitigation: Still acceptable UX, monitor P95
  • Reliability: API dependency on OpenAI → Mitigation: Fallback to current provider
  • Vendor Lock-in: Tied to OpenAI → Mitigation: Maintain abstraction layer for switching
