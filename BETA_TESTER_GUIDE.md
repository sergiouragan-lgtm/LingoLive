# LingoLive Beta Testing Guide

**Program**: Continuous Unlimited Sessions Beta  
**Duration**: 2 weeks (2026-10-01 → 2026-10-15)  
**Participants**: 5-10 language learners

---

## 🎯 Welcome to Beta Testing!

Thank you for participating in the LingoLive beta program. Your feedback will shape the future of our AI-powered language learning platform.

### What We're Testing

**Feature**: Continuous, unlimited conversation sessions with an AI language tutor.

**Key Improvements**:
- ✅ No 2-minute time limit (previously was limited to 120 seconds)
- ✅ Persistent conversation history
- ✅ Real-time session tracking
- ✅ Context-aware AI responses
- ✅ Multi-language support (Portuguese, Spanish, English)

---

## 📋 Getting Started

### Access

1. Visit: [LingoLive Beta](https://lingolive-beta.example.com)
2. Sign in with your account
3. Select your learning language
4. Click "Start Session" to begin

### First Session

1. **Start a new session**: Click the "Start" button
2. **Send messages**: Type questions or statements in Portuguese, Spanish, or English
3. **Get responses**: The AI tutor will respond in your selected language
4. **Listen to audio**: Click the audio icon to hear the AI's pronunciation
5. **Check duration**: Watch the session timer (HH:MM:SS format)
6. **End session**: Click "End Session" when done

### Expected Features

- ✅ Message counter (shows number of exchanges)
- ✅ Session duration display
- ✅ Start/End session buttons
- ✅ AI response within 2 seconds
- ✅ Audio playback of AI responses
- ✅ Session status indicator (Active/Ended)

## 🐛 Reporting Issues

### When You Find a Bug

1. **Document It**:
   - What were you trying to do?
   - What happened instead?
   - Which device/browser?
   - Screenshot/video if possible

2. **Report Via**:
   - In-app feedback button (click "?" in top-right)
   - Email: beta@lingolive.example.com
   - GitHub Issues (if technical)

3. **Example**:
   ```
   Title: Message not sending on mobile
   Device: iPhone 12, Safari
   Steps:
   1. Open app on mobile
   2. Type message "Olá"
   3. Tap send button
   4. Message doesn't appear
   
   Expected: Message appears in chat
   Actual: Button clicked but no message sent
   ```
